Month starts.

Every month start, manufacturers launch their programs. We turn those offers into complete campaigns, created from zero, live everywhere within days. Every first of the month, a new start.

The mechanics

From manufacturer offer to live campaign

  • 01

    Visual creation, from zero

    The campaign's concept and master visual, compliant with manufacturer standards and the dealership's image.

  • 02

    Per-platform declination

    Every format cut for its platform, feed, story, reel, display. The message stays, the shape adapts.

  • 03

    Website banners

    The dealership's banners updated with the month's offer, from day one of the program.

  • 04

    Organic posts

    The month's organic calendar carries the same campaign. Paid and organic tell the same story.

  • 05

    Injection into SEM and SMM

    Models on promotion join the running search and social campaigns. The whole system pushes the same offer, at the same time.

The rhythm

The program drops on the first of the month. The campaign is rolling before the competition opens the file.
