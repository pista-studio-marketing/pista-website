Feeds & API.

Campaigns wired to inventory. We connect the dealership's feeds by API so advertising updates itself, vehicle by vehicle, price by price. Inventory is your fuel.

What we operate

Inventory that advertises itself

  • 01

    Feed connection by API

    Your inventory system wired to the ad platforms. One source of truth, no more manual updates.

  • 02

    Dynamic vehicle ads

    Every in-stock vehicle generates its own ads, photos, price and details included, across search and social.

  • 03

    Continuous sync

    A sold vehicle exits the campaigns automatically. An adjusted price reflects everywhere. No budget wasted on a car that's gone.

  • 04

    Per-vehicle remarketing

    The buyer who viewed a model sees that model again, not a generic ad. The feed powers the remarketing.

The principle

When inventory moves, the campaign follows. Without being pushed.
