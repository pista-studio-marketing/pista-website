The brand

About.

An agency born in Quebec City, driven by a passion for cars and one conviction, that dealerships deserve marketing finished as beautifully as their vehicles.

The name

Pista, the track

Pista means "the track" in Italian and Spanish. The circuit. Our field. We market the brands that come from racing. And the period that follows the name reads as confident punctuation. For those in the know, it's a finish line.

The founder

Patrick Ouellette

Founded by Patrick Ouellette, Pista Studio serves French-speaking and English-speaking dealerships, from Quebec City to Ontario and New Brunswick. The agency works in both languages with the same rigour.

Marketing track-born brands is our only trade. That focus makes the difference.
