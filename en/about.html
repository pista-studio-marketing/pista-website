The brand

About.

An agency born in Quebec City, driven by a passion for cars and one conviction, that dealerships deserve marketing finished as beautifully as their vehicles.

The name

Pista, the track

Pista means "the track" in Italian and Spanish. The circuit. Our field. We market the brands that come from racing. And the period that follows the name reads as confident punctuation. For those in the know, it's a finish line.

Brand architecture

Three areas of expertise, one mission

Pista Studio is the house. Under it, three areas of expertise cover the full field of automotive marketing. Each carries the PSM monogram, for Pista Studio Marketing.

PSM Events Experience makers

Events, launches, brand experiences at the dealership and on the track.

PSM Digital Digital marketing

Paid acquisition, SEO, inventory feeds, vehicle listings, website, newsletter.

PSM Content Content and production

Content creation, social media, blogs, creator management aligned with the dealership's goals and inventory.
